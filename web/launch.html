    What the launch does

    One transaction from your test wallet to a new sub-account of it, with four actions: CreateAccount, Transfer, DeployContract and new. There is no AddKey.

    • The deposit pays for the contract code at the protocol's storage price (0.00001 NEAR per byte) plus 0.5 NEAR for the token's own state. It stays on the token account.
    • If new refuses a value, the whole transaction is undone: no account is created and the deposit comes back, less gas.
    • After the launch the page reads NEAR testnet and checks four things: the account exists, view_access_key_list is empty, get_params and ft_metadata equal what you asked, and the account's code hash equals the hash of the published wasm.
    • The wasm is built from the repository's source by its CI workflow and published next to this page with its hash.
    • LOUD limits how fast early supply can be sold on its defined path (shout, notice, leave into the built-in pool), however many wallets hold it. It does not prevent fraud. Testnet only, unaudited.
